Oil tanker means a ship constructed or
adapted primarily to carry oil in bulk
in its cargo spaces and includes
combination carriers and any “chemical
tanker" when it is carrying a cargo or
part of a cargo of oil in bulk.

Denotes registry of a vessel in a
country that does not reqire nationals
to be involved in the ownership or
operation of a vessel.

A class of vessels which, for the
purposes of this Code, is taken to be
in the range of 50,000-80,000 dwt in
size and is capable of negotiating the
Panama canal maximum beam of

32.1 metres.

The quantity of cargo carried by a
certain vessel on a particular voyage.

The term “partly filled compartment"
refers to any compartment wherein
ballast water or cargo in bulk are not
loaded to their highest possible level.
