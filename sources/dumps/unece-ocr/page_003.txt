PREFACE

Under the auspices of the ECE Working Party on Coal, this Code of Uniform
Standards and Procedures for the Performance of Draught Surveys of Coal
Cargoes has been elaborated by experts from major coal exporting and importing
countries and was completed in October 1991.

The Code was adopted by the ECE Working Party on Coal at its session in
October 1991 (ENERGY/WP.1/2, Para. 42) and endorsed by the ECE Committee on
Energy at its session in November 1991 (ECE/ENERGY/18, para. 23 (e)). The
maintenance and application of the Code will be monitored by the ECE Meeting
of Experts on Coal Trade, Statistics and Transport, in cooperation with other
interested international organizations.

NOTE: Mention of any names of commercial firms in this document is only for
reference to a process and does not imply endorsement by the
United Nations.
