Load line length, Ll, is to be taken as
96% of the total length on a waterline
at 85% of the least moulded depth
measured from the top of the keel, or
as the length from the fore side of the
stem to the axis of the rudder stock on
the waterline, if that is greater. In
ships designed with a rake of keel, the
waterline on which this length is
measured is to be parallel to the
designed waterline. The length L1 is
to be measured in metres.
